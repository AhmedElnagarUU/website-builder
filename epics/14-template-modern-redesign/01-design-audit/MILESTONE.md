# Milestone 01 — Design & Template Audit

## Goal
Analyze every template and renderer section to identify what makes the current output feel flat and generic, study modern professional websites for the *principles* that make them feel premium, and produce a binding `audit-report.md` that defines each template's design language. No code changes in this milestone.

## Tasks (execution order)
1. **01-audit-and-research.md** — Full audit of all 10 templates + external design research + per-template design-language plan → `epics/14-template-modern-redesign/01-design-audit/audit-report.md`.

## Shared context (binding for this milestone)
- **Deliverable is a document, not code.** `audit-report.md` becomes the binding design source for Milestones 02–06. Later milestones implement *its* decisions; the audit must therefore be precise and actionable (concrete tokens, layout sketches, component names), not vague ("make it nicer").
- Every design-language plan must fit the extended style model planned in M02 (themes `corporate | bold | warm | retail | creative`; fields `surface: light|deep`, `headingFont: serif|sans`, `hero: photo-bleed|split-light|split-deep`, `accentRole: fill|edge`) — the audit assigns one theme per template and explains it, it does not invent new model fields.
- Research sources allowed: Webflow templates, Awwwards, Framer templates, modern SaaS/agency/business/portfolio sites. Extract principles; **do not** copy any specific site. Record 6–10 concrete, reusable "why modern sites look professional" principles with a short justification each.
- The audit must inventory, per template: current visual problems, structural problems, missing sections, weak components, repetition, typography problems, spacing problems, responsive problems, content problems, what to preserve, what to redesign, what to make reusable.
- The five shared themes pair templates into two-template families that share a design *system* while differing in palette/hero/accent/typography (marketplace-family approach). Distinctive per family; never identical across families.
