Triage Guide

Call now for these.

Not sure if it's an emergency? Call anyway. If it can wait, we'll tell you flat — and quote you a regular visit instead.

Water leaks

Burst or active leaks, ceiling staining, flooding from any fixture. We'll shut off and stop the damage first.

No heat or cooling

Mid-January failure, elderly or infant in the house, or a smell coming from the unit — that's a call.

Electrical danger

Sparking outlets, burning smell, breaker that won't reset, warm walls. Power off at the panel if safe, then call.

Storm & structural

Tree through the roof, missing shingles mid-rain, or a sagging beam overnight. We tarp and brace what we can.
