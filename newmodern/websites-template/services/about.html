Form 01 · How it started

Started with one van and a bad experience.

Redline was founded in 2011 by two Portland locals — a HVAC tech and a carpenter — who were tired of watching neighbors get fleeced by big franchises and ghosted by solo handymen. Their rule was simple: if you wouldn't do it in your own mother's house, you don't do it here.

Twelve years later the two of them are still on jobs, now backed by fourteen licensed crews and a full-time dispatch desk. We grew by referral, in the way of a place people actually recommend.
