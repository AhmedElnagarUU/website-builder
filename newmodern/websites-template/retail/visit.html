Getting here

We're on the Mon-Fri corner of E 6th & Waller, in a building with a small kiln chimney and a big south window. The bike lanes take you right past; there's a rack out front.

Street parking is free on the side streets. The 4 and 17 buses stop at 6th & Comal, two blocks away.

Dogs are welcome if they're on a leash and clumsy about our racks. Kids are always welcome.
