Dal forno · Tonight

One thing the oven loves more than us: October air.

Comfortable weather is rare in Portland, so we cook to it. Braised lamb shoulder, soft polenta, winter squash from the Fourth Street farm — the menu shifts with what the morning market coughs up.

Il forno

Brick, fire, and forty years of patience.

Our oven was built by hand in 2014 from clay and brick salvaged from a demolished bakery our chef's grandfather walked past every childhood evening. It's been lit every day since — nobody here can remember it cold.
