How it works

One conversation, then the fire takes it from there.

Write to Rosa with the guest count and the occasion. She replies within two business days with a menu written for your night — no templates, no catering menus, no "chicken or fish" forms.

01

Tell us the occasion

Wedding, launch, birthday, retirement, "we just miss the old gang." No detail is too small — allergies, seating quirks, a song you all want.

02

We build the menu around the fire

What's in season and what the oven loves becomes a five- to seven-course menu. Signature dishes sit next to things we only make for parties.

03

30% holds the night

A simple deposit holds the date. The balance settles on the night. Cancel with 10 days' notice and you get it all back — no fine print theater.
