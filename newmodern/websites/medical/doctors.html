Co-Founders

Leadership

Dr. Sarah Chen

Co-Founder & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Chen earned her DDS from UT Health San Antonio and completed advanced training in cosmetic dentistry at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. With 15+ years of experience, she specializes in porcelain veneers, smile makeovers, and full-mouth rehabilitation. She holds accreditation from the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Cosmetic Dentistry Veneers Smile Design

Dr. Michael Torres

Co-Founder & General Dentist

Dr. Torres received his DDS from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and completed a General Practice Residency at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital. He brings 14 years of experience in restorative and preventive dentistry. He's passionate about patient education and leads our community outreach programs.
