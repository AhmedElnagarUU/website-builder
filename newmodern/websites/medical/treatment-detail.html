What Is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that uses a series of custom-made, virtually invisible clear aligners to gradually move your teeth into their ideal positions. Each set of aligners is engineered using advanced 3D computer imaging technology and manufactured from SmartTrack material — a BPA-free, medical-grade thermoplastic.

Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign aligners are removable, allowing you to eat, brush, and floss normally throughout your treatment. You'll switch to a new set of aligners every 1-2 weeks, with each set making incremental adjustments to your teeth.

At Clearview Dental, our orthodontic team has completed over 500 successful Invisalign cases, earning us Diamond Provider status — placing us in the top 1% of Invisalign providers nationwide. Whether you need minor cosmetic adjustments or comprehensive bite correction, we have the expertise to deliver outstanding results.

Benefits of Invisalign Treatment

Faster Treatment Time

Many cases are completed in 6-12 months, compared to 18-24 months with traditional braces. Mild cases may be finished in as little as 3 months.

Better Oral Health

Since aligners are removable, you can maintain your regular brushing and flossing routine throughout treatment, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.

Predictable Results

See a virtual 3D simulation of your treatment outcome before you even start. Know exactly how your smile will look when treatment is complete.

Boosted Confidence

Straighten your teeth without the self-consciousness of metal braces. Perfect for adults in professional environments who want discretion.

No Speech Disruption

Unlike some orthodontic appliances, Invisalign aligners have a minimal impact on speech. Most patients adapt within 1-2 days.

Active Lifestyle Friendly

Remove aligners for sports, photography, and special events. No risk of broken brackets or emergency orthodontic visits.

The Invisalign Process at Clearview Dental

1

Free Consultation & Digital Scan

Your first visit includes a comprehensive examination, digital impressions using our iTero scanner (no gooey impressions), and a discussion of your smile goals. We'll create a ClinCheck 3D simulation showing your projected results before you commit.

2

Custom Aligner Fabrication

Once you approve your treatment plan, your custom aligners are manufactured using SmartTrack material. You'll receive several sets at a time, along with detailed instructions on wear time (20-22 hours per day recommended).

3

Progressive Treatment

You'll switch to a new set of aligners every 1-2 weeks at home. We schedule brief check-up appointments every 6-8 weeks to monitor your progress and provide your next sets of aligners. Many patients love that these visits are quick — often just 15 minutes.

4

Retention & Follow-Up

After your active treatment, you'll receive a custom retainer to maintain your beautiful new smile. We provide complimentary retainer checks for the first year and offer a clear retainer subscription program for ongoing replacement.

Invisalign Questions

For optimal results, we recommend wearing your Invisalign aligners for 20-22 hours per day. You can remove them for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing. Consistent wear time is the most important factor in keeping your treatment on track.
You may experience mild pressure or discomfort for the first 2-3 days after switching to a new set of aligners. This is normal and indicates the aligners are working. Most patients find Invisalign significantly more comfortable than traditional braces. Over-the-counter pain relievers can help if needed.
You should remove your aligners before drinking coffee, tea, wine, or any colored beverages. Drinking with aligners in can stain them and potentially cause cavities since the liquid gets trapped between the aligner and your teeth. You can drink while wearing them only if it's plain water.
Invisalign can treat patients of any age — from teenagers (with compliance indicators) to adults. Invisalign Teen includes blue compliance indicators that fade as the aligners are worn, helping parents and dentists track usage. For younger children, we evaluate on a case-by-case basis depending on dental development.
Rinse your aligners every time you remove them. Brush them gently with a soft toothbrush and clear antibacterial soap (not toothpaste, which can scratch the plastic). Soak them in Invisalign cleaning crystals or a denture cleaning solution weekly. Never use hot water, as it can warp the aligners.
