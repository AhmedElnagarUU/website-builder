Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our practice, treatments, insurance, and patient care.

We recommend visiting the dentist every six months for a routine checkup and professional cleaning. However, depending on your oral health needs, your dentist may recommend more frequent visits. Patients with gum disease, a history of cavities, or other ongoing concerns may benefit from visits every 3-4 months. During your initial examination, we'll create a personalized recall schedule tailored to your needs.
Dental anxiety is extremely common, and our team is specially trained to help patients feel comfortable. We offer multiple sedation options including nitrous oxide (laughing gas), oral sedation, and a calming environment designed to reduce stress. Many patients find that simply communicating their concerns helps — we'll go at your pace, explain everything before we do it, and never rush you. You can also bring headphones, request breaks anytime, or schedule a no-treatment consultation visit first to meet the team.
Absolutely! We welcome patients of all ages, from toddlers to grandparents. Our family-friendly approach includes a welcoming environment for kids, gentle techniques, and preventive treatments like fluoride applications and dental sealants. We recommend children have their first dental visit by age one or when their first tooth erupts. Scheduling family appointments together can make visits more convenient and help children feel more comfortable.
You can schedule an appointment three ways: book online through our website 24/7, call us at (512) 555-0147 during office hours, or walk in during our emergency hours. Online booking is the fastest way to secure your preferred date and time. We offer morning, evening, and Saturday appointments to fit busy schedules. Same-day appointments are available for dental emergencies.
Professional in-office whitening results typically last 1-3 years depending on your diet, oral hygiene habits, and lifestyle factors. Drinking coffee, tea, red wine, and smoking can shorten the duration. We provide custom take-home touch-up kits that help maintain your brightness between professional treatments. Many patients find a quick 30-minute touch-up every 6-12 months keeps their smile looking its best.
Most adults with sufficient jawbone density and good general health are candidates for dental implants. Factors we evaluate include bone volume (assessed with 3D imaging), gum health, overall medical history, and lifestyle habits like smoking. Even patients with bone loss may qualify with bone grafting procedures. During your consultation, we'll use digital X-rays and a thorough examination to determine if implants are the right option for you.
Invisalign can effectively treat the vast majority of orthodontic cases, from simple cosmetic alignment to complex bite issues. In some severe cases involving significant jaw discrepancies, traditional braces may be recommended. During your free consultation, we'll assess your specific needs and recommend the approach that will give you the best results. As a Diamond Provider, we have extensive experience determining which option is optimal for each patient.
A standard root canal typically takes 60-90 minutes. Front teeth with a single root can be completed in about 30-60 minutes, while back teeth with multiple roots may take up to 90 minutes. Most root canals are completed in a single visit, though complex cases may require two appointments. Modern techniques and effective anesthesia mean the procedure is typically no more uncomfortable than getting a filling.
A crown (or cap) covers the entire tooth and is used when a tooth is significantly damaged, weakened by a large filling, or after a root canal. It provides full structural support. A veneer is a thin porcelain shell bonded only to the front surface of a tooth. Veneers are primarily cosmetic — they improve the appearance of teeth that are stained, chipped, slightly misaligned, or have gaps. Your dentist will recommend the appropriate treatment based on your specific dental needs.

Dental emergencies include severe toothache or swelling, knocked-out teeth, broken or cracked teeth, lost fillings or crowns, dental abscess, uncontrolled bleeding, and trauma to the mouth or jaw. If you experience any of these, call us immediately at (512) 555-0147. We reserve same-day emergency appointment slots and can often see emergency patients within hours of calling.
Time is critical. Pick up the tooth by the crown (the chewing surface), not the root. If dirty, gently rinse it with milk or saline — never scrub it. If possible, place the tooth back in the socket and bite gently on a cloth to hold it in place. If that's not feasible, store it in a container of milk or the patient's saliva.
