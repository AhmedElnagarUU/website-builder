About Clearview Dental

Our Story

Founded with a Vision for Better Dental Care

Clearview Dental was founded in 2015 by Dr. Sarah Chen and Dr. Michael Torres, two dentists who shared a common frustration: too many dental practices prioritized volume over quality, and too many patients left feeling like just another number.

They envisioned a practice where every patient felt heard, every treatment was explained thoroughly, and where advanced technology wasn't just a marketing buzzword — it was a genuine commitment to better outcomes.

Starting with a single treatment room in a modest office on Clearview Blvd, the practice grew rapidly through word-of-mouth referrals. Today, our state-of-the-art facility houses six treatment rooms, a dedicated orthodontic suite, and a team of 38 professionals who share Dr. Chen and Dr. Torres's original vision.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

Every decision we make is guided by our core values. They shape how we treat our patients, support our team, and serve our community.

Patient-First Philosophy

Your comfort, health, and goals drive every recommendation we make. We listen before we treat, and we never pressure you into unnecessary procedures.

Innovation & Excellence

We invest in the latest technology and pursue ongoing education to ensure you receive the most effective, minimally invasive treatments available.

Gentle Care

Dental anxiety is real, and we take it seriously. Our team is trained in comfort techniques, and we offer sedation options for a stress-free experience.

Community Roots

We're proud to serve the Austin community. From sponsoring local youth sports to providing free dental screenings, we give back whenever we can.

Transparency

No surprise bills, no hidden fees. We provide clear treatment plans with upfront pricing and help you navigate your insurance benefits.

Lifelong Learning

Every member of our team completes 60+ hours of continuing education annually, staying current with the latest techniques and research.
