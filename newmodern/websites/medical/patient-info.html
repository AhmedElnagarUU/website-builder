First Visit

What to Expect at Your First Appointment

We want your first visit to be comfortable and informative. Here's a preview of what happens.

1. Check-In & Paperwork

Arrive 15 minutes early to complete new patient forms (or fill them out online beforehand). We'll verify your insurance and gather your medical history.

2. Comprehensive Examination

Digital X-rays, intraoral photos, and a thorough oral cancer screening. Your hygienist will perform a detailed cleaning, and your dentist will review all findings with you.

3. Consultation & Plan

Your dentist will discuss findings, answer questions, and present a personalized treatment plan. No pressure — just honest recommendations for your oral health.

Pre-Visit Checklist

Save time at your appointment by filling out our digital new patient forms before you arrive. You'll receive a link via email after booking, or you can access them through our patient portal. This includes medical history, dental history, insurance information, and consent forms.

While we handle the verification, it helps to know your plan details. Check your annual maximum, deductible, and whether your plan covers preventive care at 100%. Our team will do a full benefits check before your appointment and let you know exactly what's covered.

This gives us time to process your paperwork, verify insurance, and get you settled comfortably. If you haven't completed forms online, you'll need this time to fill them out in our waiting area. We provide complimentary coffee, tea, and Wi-Fi.

Certain medications can affect dental treatment, especially blood thinners, bisphosphonates, and immunosuppressants. Having an accurate list helps us plan your care safely. Don't forget to include over-the-counter supplements and vitamins.

We encourage you to write down any concerns, questions, or goals you have about your smile. Whether it's about whitening, alignment, pain, or general maintenance — no question is too small. We want to understand what matters most to you.
