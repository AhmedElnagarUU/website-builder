About Us

Our Origin

Founded on Integrity

In 1987, Robert Hartwell founded Ironclad Builders with a simple principle: deliver projects on time, on budget, and with a safety record that sets industry standards. What started as a small general contracting firm in Chicago has grown into one of the Midwest's most respected construction and engineering companies.

Over the past 37 years, we have completed more than 600 projects across commercial, industrial, and infrastructure sectors. Our portfolio includes hospitals, universities, manufacturing facilities, bridges, and mixed-use developments that collectively represent over $2.4 billion in construction value.

Today, with over 800 employees and operations spanning six states, Ironclad Builders remains family-owned and deeply committed to the communities where we live and work.

Our Mission

To deliver construction and engineering solutions of the highest quality, with an unwavering commitment to safety, innovation, and the success of every client partnership.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted construction partner in the Midwest, recognized for engineering excellence, sustainable practices, and a safety culture that protects every person on every jobsite.

Safety First

With over 2.1 million hours worked without a lost-time incident, our safety record speaks for itself. Every Ironclad employee is empowered to stop work if they see an unsafe condition.
