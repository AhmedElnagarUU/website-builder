Project Overview

Building the Future of Patient Care

The Lakefront Medical Center is a 420,000 square-foot, 10-story acute care hospital and ambulatory surgical center located on Chicago's lakefront. This landmark project represents one of the largest healthcare construction projects in Illinois in the past decade.

Ironclad Builders served as the general contractor for this complex project, coordinating over 40 subcontractors across 18 months of active construction. The facility features 180 private patient rooms, 12 operating suites, an emergency department with 35 treatment bays, and a rooftop helipad.

The project required innovative construction techniques, including top-down construction to minimize disruption to adjacent hospital operations and prefabricated mechanical systems to accelerate the MEP rough-in schedule. We achieved substantial completion two weeks ahead of the contractual deadline.

Scope of Work

Our scope included site preparation, structural steel erection, exterior envelope, interior fit-out, MEP systems, medical gas, fire protection, and commissioning. The project achieved LEED Gold certification through sustainable design features including high-performance HVAC systems, low-emissivity glazing, and a green roof system.

The facility also includes a 500-space parking structure, central energy plant, and connection to the city's district steam system. Our team managed complex logistics including deliveries to a constrained urban site with limited staging areas, coordinating with three adjacent healthcare facilities.
