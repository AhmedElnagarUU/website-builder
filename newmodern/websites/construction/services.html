Our Services

Full-spectrum construction and engineering services delivered with precision, transparency, and an uncompromising commitment to safety.

General Contracting

Full-Service Delivery

Ironclad Builders provides comprehensive general contracting services for commercial, institutional, and industrial projects. We manage every aspect of construction from pre-construction planning through final occupancy.

Our experienced project managers coordinate all trades, subcontractors, and vendors, ensuring each project is delivered on schedule and within budget. We hold relationships with over 200 vetted subcontractors across the Midwest, giving our clients access to the best trades at competitive pricing.

Design-Build

Integrated Delivery

Our design-build service delivers a single-source solution where design and construction are unified under one contract. This streamlined approach eliminates finger-pointing between designer and contractor, reducing costs and accelerating project timelines.

Ironclad's in-house design team works alongside our construction professionals from day one, ensuring constructability, cost optimization, and code compliance are baked into every design decision. Our design-build projects are delivered 15-20% faster than traditional design-bid-build delivery.

Single-source accountability
Faster delivery
Cost certainty early
Value engineering

Construction Management

Expert Oversight

For complex, multi-phase projects, our construction management service provides expert oversight of schedule, cost, quality, and risk. We act as the owner's representative, managing trade contracts and ensuring project goals are met.

Our construction managers utilize advanced scheduling tools, real-time cost tracking dashboards, and weekly progress reporting to keep all stakeholders informed. This proactive approach allows us to identify and mitigate issues before they impact schedule or budget.

CM-at-Risk delivery

Pre-Construction

Planning & Analysis

Success is determined before ground is broken. Our pre-construction services include detailed cost estimating, constructability reviews, scheduling analysis, and value engineering to ensure your project starts on the right foundation.

Our estimating team uses historical data from hundreds of completed projects to deliver highly accurate budgets. We provide multiple cost scenarios, allowing owners to make informed decisions about scope, materials, and phasing before committing to construction.

Detailed cost estimating
Constructability review
Value engineering
Schedule optimization

Sustainability Consulting

Green Building

Our sustainability team guides clients through the entire green building process, from initial LEED strategy through certification. We help project teams integrate energy-efficient systems, sustainable materials, and water conservation technologies.

Ironclad Builders has achieved LEED certification on over 40 projects, including LEED Platinum and Gold designations. Our team stays current with evolving green building standards, including WELL, Living Building Challenge, and Net Zero requirements.

LEED consulting
Energy modeling
Commissioning
Life cycle analysis

BIM Services

Digital Construction

Building Information Modeling is at the center of how we plan, design, and construct. Our BIM team creates detailed 3D models that enable clash detection, 4D scheduling, 5D cost estimation, and facility management.

Using Revit, Navisworks, and Trimble technology, we detect coordination issues before they become costly field problems. Our BIM-to-field workflows ensure that what's modeled is what gets built, reducing rework and waste.

3D modeling & visualization
Clash detection
4D scheduling
5D cost integration
