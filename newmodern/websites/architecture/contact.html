FAQ

Common Questions

How do we get started with a project?

The process begins with an introductory consultation — either by phone, video call, or in person at our studio. We'll listen to your goals, answer questions about our services and fees, and discuss a possible timeline. If we both feel it's a good fit, we'll prepare a detailed proposal outlining the scope, schedule, and investment.

What is your typical fee structure?

Fees vary based on project scope and complexity. For architecture and interior design services, we typically work on a percentage of construction cost, a fixed fee, or an hourly basis depending on the nature of the engagement. We'll provide a transparent fee proposal during the initial consultation, before any work begins.

Do you work with existing buildings or only new construction?

Both. A substantial portion of our work involves renovations, adaptive reuse, and complete interior transformations of existing buildings. In fact, some of our most rewarding projects have been collaborations with historic structures where the challenge is finding new life within old walls.

Can you work with clients outside of New York?

Absolutely. Our projects span the United States, Europe, and Asia. For international and remote projects, we use a combination of video conferences, our local partner network, and in-person site visits to ensure the same level of service regardless of distance.

How do you approach sustainability?

Sustainability is integrated into every project, not treated as an add-on. We begin with passive design strategies — orientation, insulation, glazing, and natural ventilation — before considering active systems. We prioritize durable, locally sourced materials and design for long lifecycles and easy adaptation over time.
