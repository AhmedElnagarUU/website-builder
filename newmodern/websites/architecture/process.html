How We Work

Our Design Process

Architecture is a dialogue — between architect and client, between vision and reality, between the ideal and the achievable. Our process is designed to nurture that dialogue at every stage.

01

Discovery

Weeks 1–3

Every project begins with listening. We spend the first weeks understanding your vision, your lifestyle, your aspirations, and the practical realities of your site and budget. This phase involves extensive conversations, site visits, and research.

  • Initial consultations and vision workshops
  • Site analysis and environmental assessment
  • Zoning review and feasibility study
  • Program development and spatial requirements
  • Budget framework establishment
02

Concept

Weeks 4–8

With a clear understanding of your goals, we begin exploring design directions. This is where ideas take shape — through sketches, 3D models, material studies, and spatial investigations. We typically present two to three distinct concepts, each offering a different interpretation of the brief.

  • Concept sketches and spatial studies
  • 3D visualization and rendering
  • Material palette development
  • Massing and proportion studies
  • Client presentation and feedback
03

Development

Weeks 9–18

Once a concept direction is approved, we refine every aspect of the design. This is where the architecture truly comes to life — detailed plans, sections, and elevations are developed, structural systems are coordinated, and every material, fixture, and finish is specified.

  • Detailed architectural drawings
  • Structural and MEP coordination
  • Custom furniture and millwork design
  • Lighting design development
  • Material specification and sampling
04

Documentation

Weeks 19–28

The design is translated into comprehensive construction documents — the technical drawings and specifications that allow builders to execute the vision precisely. This phase ensures that every detail, from structural connections to finish transitions, is clearly communicated.

  • Full construction drawing set
  • Detailed specifications
  • Permit documentation
  • Bid preparation and contractor selection
  • Cost reconciliation and value engineering
05

Construction

Months 8–18+

During construction, we maintain close involvement to ensure the design is executed to the highest standard. Our team conducts regular site visits, reviews submittals, and coordinates with contractors to resolve any issues that arise during building.

  • Regular site observation and reporting
  • Submittal and shop drawing review
  • Quality control and craftsmanship oversight
  • Schedule and budget tracking
  • Punch list and project closeout
06

Completion

Final Weeks

The final phase is about the details that elevate a space from good to exceptional. We oversee furniture installation, art placement, lighting calibration, and landscape finishing to ensure every element works in harmony. The result is a space that feels complete, considered, and ready to be lived in.

  • Furniture and fixture installation
  • Art curation and placement
  • Lighting adjustment and calibration
  • Styling and final touches
  • Client walkthrough and handover
