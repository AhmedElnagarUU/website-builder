About Us

The Studio

Atelier Voss is a New York-based architecture and interior design studio founded in 2009 by Elena Voss and Marcus Voss. We bring together architecture, interiors, and material innovation to create spaces that are both timeless and deeply personal.

Our Story

Built on belief

The Founding Vision

In 2009, Elena Voss and Marcus Voss left their positions at Foster + Partners to establish Atelier Voss in Manhattan's Chelsea district. Their shared conviction was simple but ambitious: that architecture and interior design should not exist as separate disciplines, but as a unified practice capable of shaping every dimension of lived experience. The studio began with a single residential project in the West Village — a 1,800-square-foot loft renovation that required complete reimagining of the spatial layout, custom furniture design, and a curated material palette that referenced the neighborhood's industrial heritage.

Growth & Recognition

By 2014, the studio had grown to twelve architects and designers, completing projects across the northeastern United States. The firm's first major commercial commission — the headquarters for a media company in SoHo — earned an AIA New York Interior Architecture Award and established Atelier Voss as a studio capable of translating residential intimacy to larger scales. The following years brought commissions from hospitality groups, private developers, and cultural institutions, with the studio expanding into international work through projects in London, Tokyo, and Milan.

Today

Now a team of 24 architects, interior designers, and material specialists, Atelier Voss operates from a converted industrial space in the Meatpacking District. The studio's current body of work spans high-end residential projects across the US and Europe, boutique hospitality interiors, and a growing body of research into sustainable material systems. Every project, regardless of scale, begins with the same principle: architecture should serve the people who inhabit it, not the other way around.

Leadership

The Founders

Elena Voss

Principal Architect

Elena holds a Master of Architecture from Columbia University and a B.Arch from ETH Zurich. Before co-founding Atelier Voss, she spent six years at Foster + Partners in London, where she led the design of commercial and cultural projects across Europe and the Middle East. Her work has been published in Architectural Digest, Dezeen, and The New York Times. She is a visiting critic at Columbia GSAPP and a registered architect in New York State.

Marcus Voss

Interior Design Director

Marcus earned his degree in Interior Architecture from Parsons School of Design, followed by advanced studies in furniture design at the Royal College of Art. His twelve years of practice before Atelier Voss included roles at Roman and Williams and David Collins Studio, where he developed an expertise in hospitality and luxury residential interiors. His approach to material selection and spatial storytelling has been recognized with multiple ASID and IIDA awards.

Guiding Principles

What We Believe

Context Over Style

Every project responds to its specific place, climate, and culture. We don't impose a signature look — we listen to what a site and its inhabitants need, and let the design emerge from those conditions.

Material Honesty

We believe materials should be expressed truthfully — their texture, weight, and character celebrated rather than concealed. This honesty creates spaces with genuine warmth and lasting integrity.

Human Scale

Architecture exists to serve people. Every proportion, material, and detail is calibrated to the human body and experience. We design for how a space feels to inhabit, not just how it photographs.

Sustainable Practice

Environmental responsibility is integral to our work, not an afterthought. We prioritize passive strategies, locally sourced materials, and lifecycle thinking in every project decision.

Integrated Design

Architecture, interiors, and landscape should speak the same language. Our integrated approach ensures coherence from the building envelope to the smallest hardware detail.

Collaborative Process

Great design requires genuine partnership with our clients. We create an environment where ideas are tested, refined, and challenged until the best possible solution emerges.
