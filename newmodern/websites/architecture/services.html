What We Do

Services

From initial concept through final installation, we provide comprehensive design services that unify architecture, interiors, and material systems into a single coherent vision.

01

Architecture

Residential · Commercial · Cultural

Our architectural practice encompasses the full spectrum of building design — from single-family residences and multi-unit developments to commercial spaces and cultural institutions. We approach each project with a deep respect for context, proportion, and the lived experience of the people who will inhabit the space.

  • Concept design and feasibility studies
  • Schematic design and design development
  • Construction documentation and administration
  • Zoning analysis and permit coordination
  • Sustainable design and LEED consulting
02

Interior Design

Residential · Hospitality · Commercial

Our interiors practice creates environments that are both visually compelling and deeply functional. We develop comprehensive design concepts that encompass spatial planning, material palettes, custom furniture, lighting design, and art curation — ensuring every element contributes to a unified atmosphere.

  • Spatial planning and interior architecture
  • Material and finish selection
  • Custom furniture and millwork design
  • Lighting design and specification
  • Art advisory and styling
03

Residential Design

Private Homes · Penthouses · Estates

Our residential work is the foundation of the studio. We create homes that reflect the personalities and aspirations of their owners while responding sensitively to their sites and communities. From urban apartments to country estates, every residential project receives the same level of care and attention to detail.

  • New construction and major renovations
  • Interior architecture and fit-out
  • Landscape architecture integration
  • Smart home and technology systems
  • Full-service project management
04

Hospitality

Hotels · Restaurants · Private Clubs

Hospitality design demands a unique sensitivity to atmosphere, brand identity, and the choreography of guest experience. Our hospitality work creates spaces that feel both distinctive and welcoming, balancing visual impact with operational practicality and commercial viability.

  • Brand-driven concept development
  • Guest experience design and flow planning
  • Restaurant and bar design
  • Spa and wellness environment design
  • FF&E specification and procurement
05

Custom Fabrication

Furniture · Fixtures · Art Installations

When off-the-shelf solutions won't suffice, our in-house fabrication team designs and oversees the production of custom furniture, architectural fixtures, and art installations. This capability allows us to create truly unique environments where every element is purpose-designed for the project.

  • Bespoke furniture design and production
  • Architectural hardware and fixture design
  • Art installation and curation
  • Material prototyping and testing
  • Workshop and fabrication management
