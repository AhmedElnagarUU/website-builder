Getting Here

By Air

Santorini (Thira) National Airport (JTR) — 25 minutes by car. Direct flights from Athens, London, Paris, and major European cities during summer season.

By Sea

Santorini Port (Athinios) — 30 minutes by car. Regular ferry and high-speed connections from Athens (Piraeus) and other Cycladic islands.

Transfer Service

Complimentary luxury vehicle transfer for suite and villa guests. Please provide flight or ferry details at least 48 hours in advance.
