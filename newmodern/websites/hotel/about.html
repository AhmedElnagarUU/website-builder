Our Story

About The Meridian

Where luxury meets legend

A Vision Born from the Aegean

The Meridian was conceived from a simple yet profound belief: that luxury should not simply be experienced — it should be felt. When our founders first stood on the volcanic cliffs of Oia and gazed across the shimmering caldera, they saw not just a hotel, but the possibility of creating something that would honor the extraordinary beauty of Santorini.

Built upon the ruins of a 19th-century captain's estate, every stone of The Meridian has been carefully preserved and reimagined. The original vaulted ceilings, hand-carved doorways, and volcanic stone walls remain, seamlessly integrated with contemporary luxury and modern comforts.

Today, The Meridian stands as one of the most distinguished boutique resorts in the Mediterranean — a place where history and innovation coexist, where the ancient rhythms of island life meet the refined expectations of the world's most discerning travelers.

Our Philosophy

At The Meridian, we believe that true luxury lies in the details that others overlook. It's the warmth of genuine Greek hospitality — what we call "filoxenia" — combined with an unwavering commitment to perfection in every guest interaction.

We draw inspiration from our surroundings: the raw power of the volcanic landscape, the timeless rhythms of the Aegean, and the rich cultural heritage of the Cycladic islands. This philosophy infuses everything we do, from the locally sourced ingredients in our kitchens to the handmade textiles in our suites.
