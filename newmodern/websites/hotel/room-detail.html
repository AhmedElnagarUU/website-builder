Infinity Villa

The Infinity Villa represents the ultimate expression of luxury at The Meridian. Perched at the highest point of our property, this two-level villa offers an unparalleled living experience where indoor and outdoor spaces flow seamlessly into one another, all framed by the most breathtaking caldera views in Santorini.

The ground floor features a spacious living area with floor-to-ceiling windows, a fully equipped kitchenette, and direct access to the heated infinity pool that appears to cascade into the Aegean below. Upstairs, the master bedroom suite is a sanctuary of calm with a king-size bed, walk-in closet, and a private balcony perfectly positioned for sunrise views.
