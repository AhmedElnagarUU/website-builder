Experiences

Discover the soul of Santorini

Beyond the walls of The Meridian lies a world of wonder waiting to be explored. Our dedicated concierge team curates bespoke experiences that connect you with the culture, history, and natural beauty of Santorini — from private wine tours through volcanic vineyards to sailing expeditions along the dramatic coastline.

Wine & Culture

Volcanic Wine Tasting

Santorini's volcanic terroir produces some of Greece's most extraordinary wines. Join our sommelier for a guided journey through the island's storied vineyards, where vines grow in distinctive basket-shaped kouloura to protect them from the fierce Aegean winds.

Visit three renowned wineries, sample indigenous varietals like Assyrtiko and Vinsanto, and learn the centuries-old winemaking traditions that make Santorini a world-class wine destination. Each tasting is paired with local delicacies and accompanied by stories of the island's rich viticultural heritage.

4 Hours Up to 8 guests €180 per person

Maritime

Private Sailing

Set sail aboard a traditional wooden caïque or a modern catamaran and discover the hidden beauty of Santorini's coastline. Our private sailing experiences take you to secluded beaches, dramatic lava formations, and natural hot springs that are inaccessible by land.

Choose from half-day coastal cruises, full-day adventures including lunch on board, or the ultimate sunset sail — where you'll toast with champagne as the sun dips below the caldera in a blaze of color. Snorkeling equipment and refreshments are provided throughout.

3 – 7 Hours Up to 12 guests From €350

Culinary Arts

Greek Cooking Class

Step into the kitchen with our Executive Chef Nikos Stavridis for an immersive journey through the flavors of Greece. Begin at the local market, hand-selecting the freshest ingredients, then return to The Meridian's demonstration kitchen to master traditional and modern Greek recipes.

Learn to prepare classics like moussaka, spanakopita, and baklava alongside contemporary interpretations that reflect Chef Nikos's creative vision. The experience concludes with a leisurely lunch featuring your creations, paired with exceptional local wines.

Private Tours

Explore Santorini at your own pace with our bespoke private tour experiences, guided by local experts who bring the island's stories to life.

Full Day

Island Discovery Tour

A comprehensive private tour of Santorini with your own driver and guide. Visit the ancient ruins of Akrotiri, the iconic blue domes of Oia, the charming village of Pyrgos, and the dramatic Red Beach. Includes lunch at a traditional taverna and stops at the island's best viewpoints.

From €350 for 2 guests

Half Day

Archaeological Journey

Delve into Santorini's fascinating archaeological heritage with an expert guide. Explore the remarkably preserved Minoan settlement of Akrotiri, the ancient city of Thera on Mesa Vouno, and the prehistoric museum. A journey through 4,000 years of civilization.

From €280 for 2 guests

2 Hours

Photography Tour

Capture Santorini's most photogenic spots with a professional photographer as your guide. From the golden light of Fira's alleys to the iconic windmills and blue-domed churches, learn composition techniques while creating stunning travel photographs.

From €200 for 2 guests

Full Day

Volcanic Hot Springs

Sail to the volcanic island of Nea Kameni, hike to the active crater, then swim in the natural hot springs of Palea Kameni where geothermal waters create warm turquoise pools. A unique encounter with the living geology of Santorini.

From €420 for 2 guests
