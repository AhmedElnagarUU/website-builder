Welcome

A Sanctuary Above the Caldera

Nestled on the volcanic cliffs of Santorini, The Meridian offers an intimate escape where timeless elegance meets the raw beauty of the Aegean Sea. Our 47 luxury suites and villas are designed to frame the breathtaking caldera views that have captivated travelers for centuries.

Every detail has been thoughtfully curated — from our world-class dining to our serene spa — to create moments that linger long after you depart.
