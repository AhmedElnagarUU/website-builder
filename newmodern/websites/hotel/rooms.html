Suites & Villas

47 individually designed sanctuaries

Each of our 47 suites and villas has been thoughtfully designed to reflect the character of Santorini — from whitewashed walls and natural stone to hand-selected textiles and local artwork. Panoramic caldera views, private terraces, and bespoke amenities ensure an experience of pure indulgence.

From €890 / night

Caldera Suite

Our signature suite perches on the cliff's edge, offering unobstructed views of the caldera and the shimmering Aegean. The spacious interior features a king-size bed with premium linens, a separate living area with local artwork, and a private terrace for watching Santorini's legendary sunsets.

  • 65 sqm of living space
  • Private caldera-view terrace
  • Outdoor rain shower & soaking tub
  • Complimentary minibar & wine
