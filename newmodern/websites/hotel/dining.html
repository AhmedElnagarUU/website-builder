Culinary Excellence

Dining

Three distinctive restaurants, one unforgettable journey

At The Meridian, dining is an art form. Our three restaurants each offer a unique perspective on Mediterranean cuisine, guided by Executive Chef Nikos Stavridis and his commitment to locally sourced ingredients, time-honored techniques, and creative innovation. From sun-drenched terrace lunches to candlelit caldera dinners, every meal is a celebration.

Fine Dining

Meridian Main

Mediterranean Cuisine

Our flagship restaurant is a temple of Mediterranean gastronomy, where Chef Nikos transforms the finest local ingredients into culinary poetry. The menu celebrates the flavors of the Cyclades — from Santorini's famous cherry tomatoes and white eggplant to the freshest catch of the Aegean.

Set within our beautifully restored stone building, the intimate dining room features candlelit tables, exposed vaulted ceilings, and views into the open kitchen where our team works with quiet precision.

Dinner: 19:00 – 23:00 2 Michelin Stars

Seafood

Kai

Ocean to Table

Named after the Greek word for sea, Kai brings the bounty of the Aegean directly to your plate. Our seafood restaurant occupies a stunning waterfront position, where you can dine with your feet practically in the water as fishing boats glide past.

The menu changes daily based on the morning's catch. Expect whole grilled fish, sea urchin pasta, octopus carpaccio, and an extraordinary raw bar featuring the pristine seafood of the Mediterranean, prepared simply to let the ingredients speak for themselves.

Lunch: 12:00 – 15:00 Dinner: 18:30 – 22:30

Cocktails & Bites

The Terrace

Rooftop Cocktails & Sunset

Perched atop The Meridian with unobstructed 360-degree views, The Terrace is the most coveted spot in Santorini for watching the legendary caldera sunset. As the sky transforms through shades of gold, rose, and violet, our mixologists craft extraordinary cocktails using local spirits, fresh herbs, and house-made infusions.

The menu features refined bar bites — from Aegean shrimp skewers and truffle fries to artisanal cheese boards — designed to complement our cocktail program. Live acoustic music on select evenings adds to the enchantment.

Open: 16:00 – 01:00 Live Music

In-Room Dining

Enjoy the complete restaurant experience from the comfort of your suite or villa. Our in-room dining menu features selections from all three restaurants, available around the clock. Breakfast on your private terrace as the morning sun illuminates the caldera, or a midnight feast of fresh seafood and local wine.

In-room dining is available to all guests. For suite and villa guests, a dedicated breakfast service on your terrace is included with your stay.
