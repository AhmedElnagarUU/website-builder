Case Study

Neon Athletics

Branding & Identity — 2024

Client

Neon Athletics

Services

Brand Identity, Visual System

Year

2024

Duration

14 Weeks

The Challenge

Neon Athletics was launching into a saturated fitness market dominated by legacy brands. They needed an identity that resonated with Gen-Z athletes who value authenticity, sustainability, and bold self-expression. The existing branding felt generic — just another fitness brand in a sea of sameness.

The Solution

We built a brand that refuses to whisper. The Neon Athletics identity system is built around electric energy — bold typography, a neon-infused color palette, and a visual language that mirrors the intensity of the athletes it represents. Every touchpoint was designed to feel alive.

From the logo system and packaging to social templates and in-store experiences, we created a cohesive brand world that makes Neon Athletics impossible to scroll past.
