Observations

Six things an agency used to charge two months for.

i note to self

Real copy, not lorem ipsum.

Monomastic writes every page in your voice — about story, menu, contact note — then you edit it like a Word doc.

ii true

A site that belongs to you.

Your colors, your photos, your neighborhood. Visitors should recognize it from your Instagram.

iii both!

EN and AR, written together.

Both languages get the same care, the same rhythm, the same headlines — not a thin translation.

iv no breakage

Edits that can't break it.

Change a headline, swap a photo, add a menu item. The layout reflows. You can't mess it up.

v live

Hosted at yourname.monomastic.site.

Fast, on a real domain, ready when your customer searches for you tonight. SSL, mobile, search basics.

vi yours

Yours to keep. Always.

Export your content any time. If you outgrow us, you take the writing with you. No lock-in.

Method

Three steps. One sitting.

step 01

Tell us about your business.

A short conversation — what you sell, who comes in, what you'd want a regular to know.

Time: 8–12 minutes
Format: plain questions
You can: answer in EN or AR
step 02

Monomastic writes the site.

Every page, in both languages if you want, with your tone and your details already in place.

Time: 60–90 seconds
You see: the whole site at once
You can: regenerate, never silently
step 03

Tweak, then publish.

Edit the parts that don't sound like you. Hit publish. Send the link to your group chat.

Time: as long as you want
Hosting: included
Domain: monomastic.site or your own
