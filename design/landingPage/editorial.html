02 —What you actually get

Six things that used to take an agency two months.

i.

Written copy, not lorem ipsum.

Monomastic drafts every page in your voice — the about story, the menu descriptions, the contact note — then you edit it like a Word doc.

ii.

A site that belongs to your business.

Your colors, your photos, your neighborhood. Nothing template-shaped. Visitors should be able to tell it from your Instagram at a glance.

iii.

English and Arabic, written together.

Both languages get the same care, the same rhythm, the same headings — not a thin translation of the English version.

iv.

Edits that don't break the design.

Change a headline, swap a photo, add a menu item — the layout reflows around you. You can't mess it up.

v.

Hosted at yourname.monomastic.site.

Fast, on a real domain, ready when your customer searches for you tonight. SSL, mobile, search basics — handled.

vi.

Yours to keep. Always.

Export your content any time. If you outgrow us, you take the writing with you. No lock-in, no hostage content.

03 —How it works

Three steps. One sitting.
No tutorial required.

Ch. 01 — Answer

Tell us about your business.

A short conversation — what you sell, who comes in, what you'd want a regular to know.

Time: 8–12 minutes
Format: plain questions
You can: answer in EN or AR
Ch. 02 — Generate

Monomastic writes the site.

Every page, in both languages if you want, with your tone and your details already in place.

Time: 60–90 seconds
You see: the whole site at once
You can: regenerate, never silently
Ch. 03 — Publish

Tweak, then publish.

Edit the parts that don't sound like you. Hit publish. Send the link to your group chat.

Time: as long as you want
Hosting: included
Domain: monomastic.site or your own
