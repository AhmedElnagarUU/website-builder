Recommendation for Monomastic

Monomastic is a non-technical SMB tool, with English + Arabic as a first-class differentiator. Two variants fit best:

#1 Orange → Red (reference) — already aligned with the brand identity doc (Monomastic, “Bold & Techy,” #FF4D00 / #FF6B2C). Highest memorability, works across EN and AR, and reads as “AI that gets things done” without being developer-flavored. Use as the default production skin.

#4 Emerald → Teal — best alternative for SMB trust: a non-technical owner scanning a pricing page is more likely to click a teal CTA than a pink one. Keep it as a user-selectable accent in the editor (the brand color the PRD already exposes per site, PRD §12.2).

Skip: #2 (cliché) Skip: #5 (off-brand luxury) Skip: #6 (wrong audience) Consider: #7 (premium AR variant)
